abstract: >-
  We show that LLMs reach an in-context fact by its order of mention, not by the name in the question: a shared, steerable, low-rank subspace of fact addresses, found across 14 models from 1.5B to 32B parameters and formed early in pretraining, lets a question point to the fact it asks about while the context supplies what that fact says.
