abstract: >-
  We show that near-perfect in-distribution on-policy distillation from one or more RL-trained teachers can mask incomplete capability consolidation out of distribution, identify teacher capability and data coverage as the two bottlenecks, and propose geometry-based patching and base-preserving replay to improve out-of-distribution retention.
